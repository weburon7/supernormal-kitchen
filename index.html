BEFORE YOU ORDER

Ordering in
Ludhiana.

Where can I get delivery or pickup?

The kitchen offers delivery and pickup in Ludhiana. Message the team with your locality to confirm coverage, delivery charges and pickup instructions.

How do I preorder a meal?

Choose a dish from the food menu, then use the preorder form to prepare a WhatsApp message. The kitchen confirms availability, timing and the total price directly; the website does not complete the order.

Can I ask about a breakfast subscription?

Yes. Use the breakfast bowl plan enquiry to share your preferred bowls and days. Ask the kitchen to confirm availability, schedule and pricing before starting a recurring plan.

Do you offer event catering?

Supernormal Kitchen offers events and catering in Ludhiana. Send your event date, guest count and food preferences to the team to discuss the menu and a quote.
